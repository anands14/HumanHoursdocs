iOS

Screen Time data is missing.

Open Human Hours and allow App Protection when prompted.

You can also review access in iOS Settings › Screen Time › Apps with Screen Time Access.

Measured usage is refreshed while Human Hours is open, and widgets may take a moment to reload afterward.

Mindful Pauses are not appearing on iOS.

Open Human Hours Settings › Mindful Pauses and confirm that App Protection is allowed, a rule is enabled, and the intended app or website is selected.

Apple Screen Time enforcement requires a physical iPhone and may stop if Screen Time access is revoked in iOS Settings.

How do I delete iOS data?

Use Human Hours Settings › Clear Journal to delete every local Journal entry.

Remove individual App Protection rules or revoke Screen Time access if you no longer want Human Hours to apply them.

Delete Human Hours through iOS to remove the remaining app data stored on that device.

Choose Delete App rather than offloading, because offloading can preserve app data.

Manage any iCloud or device backup copies separately in your Apple backup settings.

Android

Mindful Pause is configured but does not appear.

Open Human Hours Settings › Mindful Pause, review the Accessibility disclosure, turn on consent, and select the app you want to watch.

Then open Android Accessibility settings and enable Human Hours Mindful Pause.

The service reads only the foreground app name and cannot read screen content.

If Android later disables the Accessibility Service, return to that system screen and enable it again.

Flow notifications or end alerts are missing.

Allow notifications for Human Hours in Android Settings › Apps › Human Hours › Notifications.

For a prompt end alert, also allow Alarms & reminders under Android Special app access where that setting is available.

Without notifications or exact alarms, Flow still runs and records locally, but off-screen reminders can be reduced or late.

Why does Android not show iOS Screen Time totals?

The Android companion does not request Usage Stats and is not a port of Apple Screen Time.

Its four primary areas are Pause, Story, Flow, and Journal.

How do I delete Android data?

Open Human Hours Settings › Delete local data to remove pauses, Journal entries, Flow sessions, Story progress, settings, and Human Hours recovery copies from that device.

You can also delete one Journal entry from its history row or uninstall Human Hours through Android.

Purchases

How do I restore a purchase on iOS?

Open Human Hours Settings, open the Pro screen, and choose Restore.

Use the same Apple Account that owns the active purchase.

How do I restore a purchase on Android?

Open Human Hours Settings and choose Restore purchase.

Use the same Google account that owns the active purchase.

How do I manage a subscription?

Manage an iOS subscription in your Apple App Store subscriptions.

Manage an Android subscription in your Google Play subscriptions.

Deleting Human Hours, choosing Delete local data, or clearing the Journal does not cancel Pro or erase store transaction history.

Privacy

How is my data handled?

Human Hours has no account system or backend that receives your wellness data.

Purchase and entitlement information is processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play and RevenueCat.

On iOS, local app data can also be carried in Apple-managed backups depending on your settings.

See the Privacy Policy for the complete iOS, Android, retention, and deletion disclosure.
